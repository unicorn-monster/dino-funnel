How I Tested Each Protein Powder

Everybody is different with different protein needs, but I wanted to be as objective as possible to find options that work for as many people as possible. 

So, I looked for protein powders with real food ingredients that taste great, feel great, and aren't full of junk.  And I especially didn’t want any that made me feel too bloated to workout.

To ensure the best ranking based not only on personal preference, but on objective factors, I tried each powder over the course of several days. This is the process I used:

  1. Found & Tried the Most Popular Brands
  2. Reviewed Ingredient Lists (And Transparency)
  3. Checked Protein Content
  4. Evaluated Sweeteners 
  5. Judged Taste and Texture
  6. Wrote Down How I Felt After
  7. Scored & Ranked Each Bar

What I Love

Equip Prime Protein feels like a luxury option. The unflavored option is made with only one ingredient: grass-fed beef protein. Even the flavored versions only add a few more like natural flavors and stevia leaf extract. That means no sugar, no fillers, and no whey. The result is a cleaner feeling protein without any bloating or discomfort.

However, the most surprising thing about Equip for me was the taste. This was the third one I tried, so I was expecting some "off" flavor like the others, but instead it created a creamy and delicious drink that tastes more like dessert than protein. No clumps, beefy taste, or chalkiness.

The fact that it's third-party tested for pesticides and heavy metals is more than just a bonus. I recently read a Harvard Health article that said many protein powders contain toxins, BPA, heavy metals, and other contaminants "with links to cancer and other health conditions." That's why I will always stick with Equip because I know it's safe.

And the best part, you can try it risk-free. If you don't like it, you can get your money back within 90 days.

What Could Be Better

Since the best price is only available on the official website, you won't be able to pick this up at the store. Luckily, you can subscribe, save money, and make sure you never run out.

But the main issue is that they frequently sell out of their most popular flavors, like chocolate and vanilla. If it's in stock, make sure you grab your favorite.
