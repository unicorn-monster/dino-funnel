Magnesium is a mineral found in every organ in your body. It is critical to bodily functions, the nervous system, and bone density. It is the most abundant mineral in the human body and has over 3,000 receptor sites.
According to Harvard University, more than 75% of adults are magnesium deficient because they aren't getting enough through their diets.

Magnesium comes in many forms. The human body digests and absorbs them differently depending on what element is bonded to it. Each supplement contains different forms of magnesium, so it is important to check the label and understand the differences.
Glycinate
: This is the best form of magnesium on the market because of its incredible absorption rate. It can be taken orally and is said to be the only one to cross the blood brain barrier - deeming it leagues above the rest. This is excellent for muscle cramps, sleep, and stress reduction.
Citrate
: Bound with citric acid, citrate is one of the cheapest and most common forms found in supplements today. Absorbable but not as much as glycinate. Good overall product.
Oxide
