# Sale Page Ad Templates (Alex Cooper 40)

Library: **SALE PAGE ads** — ads for ONE product, driving straight to its sale page. Not for Top 5 / buyer's-guide pages (that's `top5-presale.md`). IDs: SP-01..SP-40 are the Alex Cooper set; SP-41+ were added from images the user liked. Numbers are permanent: never renumber, never reuse a number. Ratio is open: every prompt ends with `[ASPECT RATIO] aspect ratio.` and the ratio is picked at gen time. "Drawn at X" on each tag line is only the ratio its % layout was written for; any other ratio re-flows per "Aspect ratios" in SKILL.md.

**Show the product in use (Huy 2026-10-23).** A viewer must see in one glance what the product is for. Wherever a template shows the product as the main visual, fill it in use with its user: a feeder with a bird on it, a pet bed with the cat or dog lying in it, a holster on a person. An empty product alone (studio, on white, on a table) is only for a pure offer / bundle / unboxing shot, and even there one unit in use beats none. Oriole feeder SP-04 and SP-05 came out as an empty feeder and read as "garden decor?".

**No brand logo anywhere (Huy 2026-10-23).** Never add a brand logo or wordmark to the frame (corner, center or divider); the product itself and the copy carry the brand. New templates leave it out too.

**No two look-alikes in one set (Huy 2026-10-23).** The Notion source had variant pairs (30/37, 13/18/35, 4/28, 7/25, 11/17, 15/24, 5/27, 20/26, 12/38) that collapsed into duplicates when all 40 were filled for one product (oriole feeder). The second of each pair was rewritten into a new mechanic (17, 18, 24, 25, 26, 27, 28, 35, 37, 38). When filling many templates for one product, also give each image its own scene, headline, stat set, competitor and person.

Templates 1–40 were written for one product type each. Copy their density and structure, never their category nouns. Every template from 41 on must pass the category swap test.
